STRIP STUDIO LOCAL — TAPE SIZE PREVIEW UPDATE

Start:
1. Extract this folder to a normal folder on your Windows computer.
2. Double-click Start-StripStudio.bat.
3. Open http://localhost:8080 in Microsoft Edge.

Changes:
- 14 mm tape is selected by default.
- 12, 14, and 15 mm tape preview options are enabled.
- Canvas height changes to represent the selected tape width.
- Fine outer guides show the tape edge; inner guides show the D30's 12 mm effective print band.
- Printing keeps the 40 mm label length and center-crops the canvas to the D30's 12 mm printable band.

Important:
- The Phomemo D30's effective print width is 12 mm. The 14/15 mm choices represent the physical tape width, not a wider printhead.
- When printing 14 or 15 mm stock, content outside the centered 12 mm printable band cannot be printed by this D30.
- Test one label after changing tape width before printing a batch.
